Inter Miami's title defense comes down to a cold night in Columbus. Lose Friday, and the defending MLS Cup champions are out of the playoffs in the first round — eliminated by the No. 7 seed before December even starts.

Game 1 wasn't close. <cite index="68-1,68-6,68-7">Inter Miami CF fell 0-3 against Columbus Crew at Nu Stadium in Game One of the Round One Best-of-3 series, with the visitors opening the scoring in the ninth minute through a penalty from Gonzalo Tapia before Columbus extended the lead with goals from Taha Habroune and Tapia again in the second half.</cite> The penalty came after <cite index="59-8">Miami goalkeeper Dayne St. Clair brought Tapia down inside the box</cite>, and things got worse from there — Miami played most of the second half a man down after a red card. Columbus finished the job comfortably, outscoring a star-studded Miami lineup that started Messi, Suárez, Rodrigo De Paul and Casemiro.

## No margin left

The format is brutal and simple. <cite index="58-6,58-7,58-8">Game 1 was Game 1 of a Best-of-3 series in Round 1 of the MLS playoffs, with Game 2 played Friday at Columbus Crew's ScottsMiracle-Gro Field.</cite> <cite index="58-9,58-10">Another Columbus win in Game 2 would see them advance to the next round, while Inter Miami would be eliminated if they lose Friday.</cite> Win, and the series shifts back to South Florida. <cite index="66-1,66-2">Game 2 is scheduled for Friday, November 27, at 8 p.m. ET in Columbus, and a Game 3 would be played at Nu Stadium on December 2 if the series is level after Game 2.</cite>

Suárez didn't hide from the stakes this week. <cite index="60-6">"It's clear that at a club like this, we have an obligation to win, getting eliminated on Friday would be a disappointment, and that's how we should see it," Suárez said on Wednesday.</cite> He went further, telling reporters <cite index="60-9">"Every single player needs to take that responsibility. Everyone knows what they can contribute this Friday; we need 150%, even 200%, from every one of us to bring this series back home."</cite> Casemiro was just as blunt about what's at stake: <cite index="59-12">"Inter Miami always plays to win and we know if we lose the next game, we are out," Casemiro said.</cite>

## The history working against them

This isn't new territory for Miami, and that's the uncomfortable part. The club has been here before with a loaded roster and an early exit to show for it — <cite index="76-7,76-8">Columbus struck first against a star-studded Inter Miami side that was memorably sent packing in Round One of the playoffs two years ago by Atlanta United.</cite> Friday adds another layer of history against them specifically with Columbus: <cite index="62-5">this will mark the second time the Crew can eliminate Miami from a major competition in Columbus, after knocking them out of the 2024 Leagues Cup thanks to a 3-2 victory at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field in the round of 16.</cite>

The road record doesn't help the case either. <cite index="62-6,62-7">Miami has never won the second game in a best-of-three MLS playoff series, and the reigning league champions have lost four of their five all-time playoff outings away from home, with their only triumph coming at FC Cincinnati in 2025 (4-0).</cite> Columbus, meanwhile, arrives feeling good about its building: <cite index="63-11">Laurent Courtois' side, which won eight of their final nine regular-season games, is in the driver's seat with Game 2 at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field, where they've won their last four matches.</cite>

None of this is really about seeding or form, though. Miami was the No. 2 team in the East. Columbus snuck in at No. 7. The gap in the regular-season standings was real, and it hasn't shown up on the field through 90 minutes of this series. A red card compounded a bad start in Game 1, but the larger issue is a team that looked rattled, not unlucky. Suárez called it correctly this week — there's no alibi available anymore. A champion with this much talent doesn't get to point at seeding when it's getting outplayed by a seventh seed on its own field.

Friday decides whether that talent gets one more chance at home or whether 2026 ends in Columbus before the calendar turns to December. The winner of the series moves on to face either New England or Philadelphia in the conference semifinals. For Miami, there's no round after this one to plan for until Friday's result is in.
